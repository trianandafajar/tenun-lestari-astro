Well-cared-for woven cloth can be handed down. The good news is that looking after it is simpler than you might think. Here are five small habits we suggest to every customer.

## 1. Don't wash it too often

Cloth that is displayed or worn occasionally only needs airing. Washing too often is the main reason natural colours fade.

## 2. Cold water and mild soap

When it does need a wash, use cold water and a bleach-free soap. Soak briefly, press gently, then rinse. Avoid wringing or scrubbing, which can pull threads.

## 3. Dry it in the shade

Direct sunlight speeds up the fading of turmeric and soga. Spread the cloth in a breezy, shady spot, inside out.

## 4. Roll, don't fold

Folding along the same lines for years can leave permanent creases. Roll wide cloths around a cardboard tube wrapped in cotton, and change the fold of scarves now and then.

## 5. Let it breathe

Avoid airtight plastic bags. Cotton wrapped in another cloth or acid-free paper is better protected from mould. Some artisans tuck in dried pandan leaves as a natural freshener.

If your cloth is already damaged, say a pulled thread or a fraying edge, please don't trim it yourself. Our repair service is happy to help.
