"Fair" is easy to print on a label and hard to prove. So we try to make our supply chain traceable, step by step.

## Yarn with a known origin

We buy cotton yarn from local spinners and record where each batch comes from, so we know which yarn went into which production run.

## Prices agreed up front

Per-piece prices are agreed with each weaving group before production starts, taking into account size, motif complexity and working time. Part of the payment is made in advance.

## A name on every label

Every product carries a card with the weaver's name and village. For us this is not just a marketing story but a form of accountability: if there is a quality issue we know who to talk to, and if a customer is happy, the praise reaches the right person.
