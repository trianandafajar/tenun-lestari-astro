Fast fashion taught us to buy a lot and buy often. Traditional weaving works the other way round: a single cloth can take weeks, and it is made to be used for years.

## Time as a raw material

In ikat weaving, time matters as much as yarn. Tying the motif, dyeing again and again, and weaving thread by thread can't be rushed without losing quality. Slow fashion values that time instead of squeezing it.

## Fewer, better things

For buyers, slow fashion means choosing fewer things you truly love and look after. For artisans, it means steadier orders and prices that reflect their work.

## The role of small brands

Brands like Tenun Lestari (fictional) can act as a bridge: keeping quality standards, telling the story behind each cloth, and making sure most of the value goes back to the village.
