Every weaving technique leaves recognisable traces. Knowing the technique also helps you choose the right care.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Ikat">
    The motif is made by tying off sections of yarn before dyeing, so its edges
    look slightly blurred. Wash indigo ikat separately for the first few washes.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Lurik">
    Stripes formed by arranging coloured threads. Light and easy to care for;
    ideal for scarves and everyday clothing.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Songket">
    A gold supplementary weft forms raised motifs. Avoid rubbing or ironing
    directly on the motif; iron from the reverse.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Anyaman">
    Threads cross in pairs like a woven mat. Dense and strong, ideal for
    tablecloths and runners.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Reading the product label

Every Tenun Lestari piece comes with a card listing the technique, the dyes used, the weaver's name and their village. Keep it: the details are useful if the cloth ever needs repair.
