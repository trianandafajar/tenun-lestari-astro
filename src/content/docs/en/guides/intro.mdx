Tenun Lestari cloth is woven by hand and coloured with natural dyes such as indigo, turmeric, soga and noni. The result is a living fabric: its colour may shift a little over time, and every piece has its own character.

This guide helps you:

- understand handwoven cloth and natural dyes,
- recognise the motifs and techniques in our collection,
- wash and store cloth so it lasts for years,
- know when to use our repair service.

## Handwoven vs. factory-made

Factory fabric is woven at an even tension and printed with stable synthetic colours. Handwoven cloth varies slightly in density and tone, because every thread is dyed and placed by hand. That variation is normal, not a defect.

## Natural dyes

Natural dyes are gentler on the environment but more sensitive to sunlight, heat and harsh detergents. With a few simple habits, their colours can age beautifully.

<Aside type="tip">
  Wash new cloth separately the first time. A little indigo in the first rinse
  water is completely normal.
</Aside>
