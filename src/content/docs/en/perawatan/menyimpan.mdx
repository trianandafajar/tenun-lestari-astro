How you store cloth matters as much as how you wash it. Well-stored cloth can last for decades.

## Roll, don't fold

Roll wide cloths around a cardboard tube wrapped in cotton. For folded scarves and shawls, change the fold every few months so it doesn't leave a mark.

## Let it breathe

- Wrap cloth in cotton or acid-free paper, not airtight plastic.
- Keep it in a dry cupboard out of direct sunlight.
- Tuck in dried pandan leaves or sandalwood as a natural freshener and moth deterrent.

## Check it now and then

Take cloth out a few times a year, air it, and check for stains, mould or pulled threads.
