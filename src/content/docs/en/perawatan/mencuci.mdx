Woven cloth doesn't need frequent washing. Pieces that are displayed or worn occasionally only need airing. When a wash is needed, follow these steps.

<Steps>

1. Prepare cold water and a mild soap without bleach or optical brighteners.

2. Soak the cloth for 5–10 minutes. Never leave it overnight.

3. Press the cloth gently. Avoid rubbing, brushing or wringing.

4. Rinse in cold water until the water runs clear.

5. Roll the cloth in a dry towel to absorb water, then lay it flat in the shade, inside out.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
  Don't dry turmeric- or soga-dyed cloth in direct sunlight. The colour will
  fade much faster.
</Aside>

## Can it go in the machine?

Only for products whose label says so, such as the Anyaman Kapas table runner. Use a laundry bag, a gentle cycle and cold water.
